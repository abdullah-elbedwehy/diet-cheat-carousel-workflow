# Identity guide

Two identities. Never mix marks or palettes between them.

## OLD — white shield

Refs attached to every worker: `assets/OLD-shield-logo.png` (exact mark), `assets/OLD-example.png` (finish reference).
Extra refs on request only: `assets/OLD-identity-sheet.png` (palette sheet), `assets/client-approved-layout.jpg` (two-choice comparison).

Palette
- Base: exact solid navy `#12181D`, matte, no grain, vignette, gradient, or radial shading.
- Copy: white.
- Warning / heavy emphasis / "higher" side: gold-orange `#F2A33A`.
- Positive / smart / "better" side: cyan-blue `#4FB3E8`.
- Restrained burgundy `#5A1F2B` only for risk panels.
- Decoration: sparse thin angular gold lines at corners, low glow, matte surfaces.

Logo: white shield, once, top-right corner, ~8% of width, clear safe space. Never on products, never duplicated, never recolored.

## NEW — hands and heart

Refs attached to every worker: `assets/NEW-logo-light.png` (exact mark), `assets/NEW-example.png` (finish reference).
Extra refs on request only: `assets/NEW-palette.jpg`, `assets/NEW-typography.jpg`, `assets/NEW-brand-guidelines.pdf`, `assets/client-approved-layout.jpg`.

Palette
- Navy `#091521`, charcoal `#222021` bases.
- Orange `#F58A29` for emphasis / warning / "higher" side.
- Platinum grey `#E8E8E8` and white for copy.
- Positive side: subdued deep blue / navy tint. No neon cyan.
- Restrained burgundy for risk.

Logo: white hands with orange heart, once, top-right, ~8% width. No fake wordmark. Never the shield.

## Shared visual language (both)

- Photoreal hero object(s): food / drink / product on a matte black plate or surface, top-down or 3/4, warm key light, cinematic but clean.
- One dominant hook in bold geometric Arabic display type; secondary lines lighter; numbers large and clean.
- Accent-colored word inside the headline for the key term (as in `الفرق هنا في الزيت والتغطية` → `الزيت والتغطية` in gold).
- Prefer negative space to boxes. Decoration lives at corners and under the information layer.
- Anonymous products only: no brand labels, no invented packaging text, no watermarks.
- No people, hands, or faces unless the copy needs them.
